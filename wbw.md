### WBW (WORD BY WORD)

A free dictation trainer. Listen, type what you hear, check accuracy, then drill the words you miss. Dictation is a compact way to work listening, spelling, reading and writing at once — this one turns that loop into a playful practice session. Best on Mac and iPad.
